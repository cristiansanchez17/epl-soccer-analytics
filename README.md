# epl-soccer-analytics

## Introduction

For this semester project for Advanced Data Analytics, I want to demonstrate my skills and knowledge for one of my favorite interests. I'm a huge soccer fan, and the English Premier League is my favorite soccer league. Consisting some of the best soccer clubs, players, and managers, the Premier League is considered the toughest league in the world. However, there are questions surrounding its competitiveness, overall league talent, and performance against European clubs. In this project, I want to analyze the overall Premier League to understand the overall trends. I also analyze other leagues including Spain to compare with the Premier League in terms of competitiveness. The goal for this project is to confirm if the Premier League is the toughest soccer league in the world.

The CSV file for this project is [combined_football_data.csv] (https://github.com/cristiansanchez17/epl-soccer-analytics/blob/main/combined_football_data.csv).
